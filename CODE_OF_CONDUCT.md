# Code of conduct

JobGlass contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and productive.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- being considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and accessibility needs;
- giving specific, technical, and actionable feedback;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- protecting private machine data found in logs, fixtures, screenshots, or exports;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and evidence rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, sexualised conduct, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of private information, or retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not include secrets or unrelated sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. A person who is the subject of a report will not decide its outcome.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
