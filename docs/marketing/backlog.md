# Prioritised marketing backlog

This is a recommendation queue. Completing an item still requires repository review and any relevant owner, release, privacy or platform approval.

## P0 - proof and boundaries

- [ ] Prove the current release or beta state and capture a content-free receipt.
- [ ] Review every proposed claim against the shipping experience, privacy behaviour and support route.
- [ ] Interview or observe five relevant people before committing to a broader channel.

## P1 - message and conversion

- [ ] Create one focused landing or listing draft around the primary job and objection.
- [ ] Prepare truthful workflow screenshots with fictional data and matching captions.
- [ ] Run one bounded distribution experiment with a named audience, conversion event and stop rule.

## P2 - scale only after evidence

- [ ] Review search-query and referral evidence after a meaningful observation window.
- [ ] Expand content or community distribution only when activation and support quality remain acceptable.
- [ ] Consider paid acquisition only with a verified conversion path, retention signal, budget cap and stop rule.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Live listing, title, subtitle, keyword, price or advertising changes
- Visibility or archive-state changes
- Publishing unreviewed content, designs or customer material
- Claims of market proof, compliance, safety or outcomes without evidence
