## Positioning

**Confirmed repository fact:** See what runs next. JobGlass is a local-first, read-only desktop view of native scheduled jobs across macOS, Linux and Windows. It is currently under v0.1.0 development from an approved specification.

**Recommendation:** jobglass is for developers and operators who want a read-only local view of native scheduled jobs across desktop platforms. It should be positioned around its narrowest proven job, with repository-backed boundaries made as visible as its benefits.

## Audience and priority use cases

**Primary audience:** Developers and operators who want a read-only local view of native scheduled jobs across desktop platforms.

- See what native jobs run next
- Inspect deterministic diagnostic reasons
- Compare platform scheduling concepts without granting write access

**Unknowns to validate:** audience urgency, current alternatives, willingness to adopt or pay, acquisition source, and the smallest credible conversion event.

## Differentiation and objections

## SEO, content and distribution opportunities

- Create one evidence-backed landing page centred on the primary job, not a feature catalogue.
- Publish two or three query-matched articles or demos that answer a real pre-purchase question.
- Use the narrowest credible channel first: existing audience, relevant community, TestFlight or direct outreach. Expand only after conversion evidence.
- Keep support, privacy and release-proof links beside every conversion action.

### Query themes to validate

- The primary job expressed in the audience's language
- The current workaround plus its most costly failure mode
- A privacy, evidence, offline, approval or recovery differentiator that the repository can prove
- The product or repository name only after non-branded intent is covered

Google gives no indexing or ranking guarantee. Search recommendations should be measured through impressions, clicks, click-through rate and qualified conversion, not assumed traffic.

## Conversion path and launch gates

1. **Proof:** verify the current build, workflow, screenshots, support route, privacy claims and release state.
2. **Message test:** show the proposed positioning to at least five relevant people and record objections in neutral language.
3. **Small conversion:** ask for one bounded action such as reading the case study, joining an approved beta, installing a verified release, or requesting a demo.
4. **Retention proof:** define the behaviour that would show recurring value before buying traffic.
5. **Scale gate:** broaden distribution only after the previous steps produce evidence and support capacity is known.

## Measurement

| Stage | Primary measure | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Qualified impressions by non-branded query or relevant referral | Do not optimise for raw reach |
| Consideration | Product-page or documentation engagement | Exclude owner and test traffic where possible |
| Conversion | Completed approved next step | Separate configured links from genuinely joinable or purchasable states |
| Activation | First successful core workflow | Define from product evidence before instrumentation |
| Retention | Repeat core workflow in an appropriate interval | Avoid vanity streaks or coerced notifications |
| Quality | Support burden, failure rate, refunds or uninstalls | Do not scale acquisition while this worsens |

## Claim guardrails

- The repository is under v0.1.0 development, not a finished release.
- Do not imply full scheduler or platform coverage before support-matrix proof.
- Never claim JobGlass repairs, disables or manages jobs.
- Label roadmap, beta, recommendation and hypothesis language explicitly.
- Avoid best, leading, guaranteed, secure, compliant, effortless and similar unbounded claims.
- Revalidate every platform-policy statement and live URL immediately before publication.
